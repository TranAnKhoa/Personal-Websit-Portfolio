About

Dao Thi Thanh Binh has completed her PhD at the age of 36 years from Ho Chi Minh City University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Viet Nam. She was the former Head of Cardiovascular department of Nguyen Trai hospital with nearly 30 years of experience specializing in arrhythmia and cardiology. Following comprehensive faculty development training led by Professors Hoi Ho and Ho Kim Tuyen from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center El Paso, as part of a collaborative educational program between the two institutions, she has chosen to immerse herself in medical education. She now serves as the director of the Center for Medical Education as well as a senior lecturer of Cardiology at Pham Ngoc Thach University of Medicine in Vietnam and General Secretary of Ho Chi Minh City Heart Rhythm Society.
